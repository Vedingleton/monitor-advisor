How the numbers work

No affiliate ranking or vendor bias — the scoring only cares about the hardware.

01

Pick your parts

Choose a CPU and GPU (or your GPU and a target resolution) from the dropdowns — no account, no install.

02

We normalise the benchmarks

Every part is scored against the dataset using z-score normalisation, so a budget CPU and a flagship GPU are compared fairly.

03

Get a straight answer

A single verdict — which part is the limiter, and roughly how much performance you're leaving on the table.

Built by someone who kept getting this wrong

PCRigCheck started as a way to stop guessing whether a part upgrade was actually worth it. The bottleneck checker exists because most online calculators use flawed benchmark methodology — this one doesn't.

More tools and component write-ups are on the way as the dataset grows.
